Contest rules are in `docs/JUDGING.md`: 3 to 5 minutes, names and affiliations on screen, under 300 MB, and the video must say which techniques were created, adapted or extended. The contest deadline for the abstract and video is 16 December 2026. The hackathon demo on 9 November only needs the backup recording.
